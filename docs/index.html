Two ways to play

The same golf, however you swing

The same courses, games, challenges and friends either way. Start as a game tonight; plug in a launch monitor when you have one.

As a golf game

No launch monitor needed.

  • Swing with a finger on your phone, or a mouse or game controller on your PC: pull back, push through.
  • Pick your level, Beginner to Tour: forgiving and shorter, or demanding and long. Two good rounds and you move up.
  • Full rounds on the courses that come with it, the range, the greens and the challenges.
  • Play friends online, on their phones and PCs.

With your launch monitor

Open Connect launch monitors, in your bay.

  • Your real swing: each shot's ball speed, launch and spin fly the ball.
  • Every number on the picture, or on a second screen of their own.
  • Stats from your real shots: handicap, strokes gained, every club's distance, and tips from your own misses.
  • Swing cameras with instant replay, and bay view for a projector.

What's in it

More golf than one bay can hold

Everything here works whichever way you swing.

Play friends online

  • Up to 8 players, on their own PCs and phones, across the house or across the country.
  • Nobody waits a turn: everyone hits when they're ready, so a round takes about an hour.
  • Stroke play, Stableford, match play and skins, off handicaps. Better ball and scramble for teams.
  • Match ladders to climb, and golf pong on a real green.

Design your own course

  • A whole course in a few clicks: parkland, woodland, lakes or links, flat to mountain.
  • Type in a scorecard and every hole is laid out to match it.
  • Or play a real course from open map data, on its real ground with its real trees.
  • Change any hole: brush the fairway wider, drag the green, move the pin, add bunkers, water and trees, then play it.
  • Holes in the style of the great courses: Redans, Edens, Biarritz greens, the Alps.

Practise and get better

  • A putting green that reads the break and tells you what to change: the pace or the line.
  • A driving range with a tracer, every number, and a session view of every ball.
  • With a launch monitor, stats that matter: handicap, strokes gained, every club's distance, and tips drawn from your own misses.
  • Challenges and ladders with a score to beat, and a plan for what to practise next.
  • Your level and skill points grow as you play.

Play together

Friends play friends, however they swing

One online game can hold a bay with a launch monitor, a friend on a phone, and another on the sofa with a controller. Everyone hits when they're ready, from wherever they are.

Launch monitor shots count in your stats and handicap. Swings with a finger, mouse or controller are for the game, at the level each player picks.

  • Launch monitorA real club and ball, in the bay
  • TouchA finger on the phone's swing pad
  • Game controllerThe right stick, back and through
  • MouseA pull and a push on the PC

Get it

On the tee in five minutes

  1. Download the installer

    A-Squared-Golf-Setup from the downloads page: the newest stable version is at the top.

  2. Run it

    Windows may say it doesn't know the publisher yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway. It installs for your user, with no admin needed.

  3. Hit a shot

    With a launch monitor, the welcome screen checks it's talking. Without one, swing with a game controller's right stick, or turn on the mouse swing (Menu, Swing pad & controller). From then on A² Golf keeps itself up to date.

On an Android phone

A² Golf on your phone, swung with a finger: rounds, the range and the greens, and online games with friends on their phones and PCs. It's a test version, installed from the file rather than the Play Store.

  1. On the phone, tap Download for Android below.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android asks to allow your browser to install apps: allow it, then tap Install. If Play Protect says it doesn't know the app, choose More details, then Install anyway.
  3. Open A² Golf. When a newer version is out, the app says so: download it the same way and it installs over the top, keeping your rounds.

Privacy
A-SQUARED GOLF - PRIVACY NOTICE

Short version: the game has no accounts and no adverts, and it doesn't
track you. Your shots, stats, recordings and courses stay on your own
computer. It only sends something over the internet when you use a
feature that needs to, as listed below.

WHO WE ARE
A2 Games, the makers of A-Squared Golf ("we"). Contact us through Menu ->
System -> Feedback in the game, or
https://github.com/apashton/Golf-Sim-releases/issues

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR COMPUTER
- Players' names, shots, stats, rounds and scorecards.
- Settings, courses you make or import, hole edits.
- Webcam replays, recordings and pictures.
- Logs of the launch monitor connection, kept to help fix problems.
These are in the game's own folder on your PC (System -> When something's
stuck shows where). We never see them unless you send them to us. Removing
the game and that folder removes them.

WHAT IS SENT, AND WHEN

1. Online play (only when you host or join an online game).
   The game's code, the players' names, the shots and scores of the
   game, and messages and reactions in it are passed between the bays
   through our relay, run on Cloudflare. The relay keeps nothing about a
   game once its players have left. Your internet address is seen by
   Cloudflare while you're connected, as with any website. Online match
   ladders keep what their players report - names, challenges, scores and
   rounds - for as long as the ladder is in use.

2. Linking a phone (only when you link one).
   Stats from a linked phone wait on the relay until your PC collects
   them, then they're removed from there. The PC leaves its players'
   names, handicaps and stats summed up there for its phones to collect.
   Ending the link from the PC removes it and everything waiting on it.

3. Updates.
   The game asks GitHub (github.com) if there's a new version, and
   downloads it from there. GitHub sees your internet address, as with any
   download.

4. Course maps (only when you import a course or search for a place).
   The place you search for and the area you import are sent to public
   map services: OpenStreetMap's search and data services (Nominatim,
   Photon, Overpass), and ground height services (such as the
   Environment Agency, Scottish and Welsh Government LiDAR, the US
   National Map, and Amazon's open terrain tiles). They see your internet
   address and the area, under their own privacy policies.

5. Feedback (only when you choose to send it).
   Menu -> System -> Feedback saves a report on your PC - your note, the
   version, your settings, the screens, the graphics card, the launch
   monitor's connection log and recent errors - and opens a page where you
   can post it. Read it before you post: on GitHub's issues it is public.
   Nothing is sent until you post it yourself.

6. Online play from other houses with Tailscale (only if you set it up).
   Tailscale is a separate program with its own privacy policy.

7. The website (apashton.github.io/Golf-Sim-releases).
   It counts visits and which of its links are clicked (the downloads,
   the trailer) with GoatCounter, which sets no cookies and keeps nothing
   that says who you are: the page, where you came from, your browser's
   kind and screen size, and your country, counted. GitHub hosts the
   site and the downloads, and counts how often each download is taken.

WHAT WE DON'T DO
- No accounts, no adverts, no tracking. The game itself counts nothing
  about you; the website counts visits, as above.
- We don't sell or share anything about you.
- Voice commands are heard by Windows' own speech recogniser on your PC;
  no sound leaves your computer.
- Webcams are used only on your PC, for replays and recordings.

CHILDREN
The game has no accounts and collects nothing about who you are. Online,
players see the names you type in: use a nickname if you'd rather.

YOUR RIGHTS
If you live in the UK or the EU you have rights over data about you
(to see it, correct it, or have it removed). As almost everything stays on
your own PC, you can see and remove it yourself. For anything held on the
relay (an online ladder), contact us and we'll help.

CHANGES
If this notice changes, the new one comes with the update that changes
it, and is in Menu -> Help -> About.
