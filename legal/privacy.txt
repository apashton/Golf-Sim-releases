A-SQUARED GOLF - PRIVACY NOTICE

Short version: the game has no adverts, and it doesn't sell or share
anything about you. You sign in so A² Golf knows who you are on this
device and on the Tour. Your shots, stats, recordings and courses stay
on your own computer (and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive).
It only sends something over the internet when you use a feature that
needs to, as listed below.

WHO WE ARE
A2 Games, the makers of A-Squared Golf ("we"). Contact us through Menu ->
System -> Feedback in the game, or
https://github.com/apashton/Golf-Sim-releases/issues

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR COMPUTER
- Players' names, shots, stats, rounds and scorecards.
- Settings, courses you make or import, hole edits.
- Which accounts are signed in on this device, kept beside the stats.
- Webcam replays, recordings and pictures.
- Logs of the launch monitor connection, kept to help fix problems.
These are in the game's own folder on your PC (System -> When something's
stuck shows where). We never see them unless you send them to us. Removing
the game and that folder removes what's on this computer, but not an
account or anything already on the Tour or a ladder (see Your rights).

WHAT IS SENT, AND WHEN

1. Signing in (asked the first time A² Golf opens, on the PC, the phone
   or the web version).
   Signing in is through Google's Firebase, on the A2 Golf project:
   Continue with Google, or an email and a password. With Google, your
   email and name as Google has them are used to sign you in. Friends,
   children and guests can play on this device without a login of their
   own. After you sign in, the game opens as you every time, including
   offline. Signing out on a device does not delete the account, or
   what's already on the Tour or a ladder.

2. Online play (only when you host or join an online game).
   The game's code, the players' names, the shots and scores of the
   game, and messages and reactions in it are passed between the bays
   through our relay, run on Cloudflare. The relay keeps nothing about a
   game once its players have left. Your internet address is seen by
   Cloudflare while you're connected, as with any website. Online match
   ladders keep what their players report - names, challenges, scores and
   rounds - for as long as the ladder is in use.

3. The Tour (only when you play the Tour).
   Your account is known to the relay only as a hash of its id, never
   your email. Your scores, your cards, and the name you choose to show
   (your player name in the game, or "A player") are kept for the week's
   boards and the season's table. Those boards are public, on the web
   too.

4. Linking a phone (only when you link one).
   Stats from a linked phone wait on the relay until your PC collects
   them, then they're removed from there. The PC leaves its players'
   names, handicaps and stats summed up there for its phones to collect.
   Ending the link from the PC removes it and everything waiting on it.

5. Stats in your Google Drive (only if you sign in for them on My stats).
   The player showing's stats are kept in your own Google Drive, in a
   folder A² Golf makes, so they can follow you to another computer.
   A² Golf can only see the files it made, never anything else in that
   Drive. Signing out stops the sync; it does not delete the files.
   You can take A² Golf's access back from Google's Security ->
   Third-party access page, and remove the A² Golf folder in Drive
   yourself.

6. Updates.
   The game asks GitHub (github.com) if there's a new version, and
   downloads it from there. GitHub sees your internet address, as with any
   download.

7. Course maps (only when you import a course or search for a place).
   The place you search for and the area you import are sent to public
   map services: OpenStreetMap's search and data services (Nominatim,
   Photon, Overpass), and ground height services (such as the
   Environment Agency, Scottish and Welsh Government LiDAR, the US
   National Map, and Amazon's open terrain tiles). They see your internet
   address and the area, under their own privacy policies.

8. Feedback (only when you choose to send it).
   Menu -> System -> Feedback saves a report on your PC - your note, the
   version, your settings, the screens, the graphics card, the launch
   monitor's connection log and recent errors - and opens a page where you
   can post it. Read it before you post: on GitHub's issues it is public.
   Nothing is sent until you post it yourself.

9. Online play from other houses with Tailscale (only if you set it up).
   Tailscale is a separate program with its own privacy policy.

10. Phone alerts (only if they're turned on, and only when you allow them).
    A challenge, an answer or a Postal round on a ladder can be sent to
    your phone, even with the app closed, through Firebase Cloud
    Messaging. The app asks once whether it may. Until the feature is
    set up, the app never asks and no alert is sent.

11. The website (apashton.github.io/Golf-Sim-releases).
    It counts visits and which of its links are clicked (the downloads,
    the trailer) with GoatCounter, which sets no cookies and keeps nothing
    that says who you are: the page, where you came from, your browser's
    kind and screen size, and your country, counted. GitHub hosts the
    site and the downloads, and counts how often each download is taken.

WHAT WE DON'T DO
- No adverts, no selling or sharing of anything about you.
- The website counts visits and link clicks as above, with no cookies.
  The game itself has no advertising, and no extra tracking beyond
  what's listed here.
- Voice commands are heard by Windows' own speech recogniser on your PC;
  no sound leaves your computer.
- Webcams are used only on your PC, for replays and recordings.

CHILDREN
Children and guests can play without a login of their own, as players on
a signed-in person's device. Online, people see the names you choose to
show: use a nickname if you'd rather.

YOUR RIGHTS
If you live in the UK or the EU you have rights over data about you
(to see it, correct it, or have it removed). Almost everything stays on
your own PC, and you can see and remove that yourself. For an account,
Tour or ladder entries, or anything else we hold, contact us through
Menu -> System -> Feedback or the support page above, and we'll help.
Signing out on a device does not delete your account or those records.

CHANGES
If this notice changes, the new one comes with the update that changes
it, and is in Menu -> Help -> About.
