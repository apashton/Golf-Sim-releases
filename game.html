Saved players

Everyone who keeps stats here: a name, a colour, which hand they play with (a left-hander's draw and fade are the other way round in the tips), a handicap index, their level with the mouse or a game controller (Beginner to Tour - blank plays at the menu's) and Simple for young children - their shots are kept, but nothing asks about them. Renaming someone takes their stats with them; removing someone keeps their stats.
