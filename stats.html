Clubs full swings

Typical: the middle 80% of your shots. Left / right: where the middle 80% finish. The arrows compare your last 20 shots with the ones before.

Thin, fat, topped: how often each club's full swings are struck that way. They're counted here but left out of the club's carry and everything else above, as they're not what you'd expect the club to go - and so are shots taken out as misreads, which count nowhere.

Special shots - a punch, a part swing or anything else on purpose - count nowhere here: not in the carry, not in thin / fat, not in the gapping or the tips. They're listed under Special shots below.

Ball speed: how fast the ball leaves the club. Launch: how steeply it takes off. Spin: backspin, in turns a minute - more makes it climb and stop, less makes it run. The way a shot curves comes from the ball's spin being tilted, like a spinning top leaning over.

Games and rounds

Rounds course rounds, and any play recorded as Round

Fairways: tee shots on par 4s and 5s that finished on the fairway. Greens: on the green (or in) in par less two strokes, penalties counted. Putts: strokes from the green - a gimme counts as one. Up and down: after missing the green, in the hole in two from the first chip or pitch. Penalties: water and lost balls. SG: strokes gained against the golfer chosen below (your handicap to start with). Diff: the round's handicap score differential; net: the score less the course handicap it was played off. Every stroke counts as it did on the scorecard, a misread too.
